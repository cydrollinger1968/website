Health Monitoring Smart Ring by Smart Ring Solutions

Revolutionary Health Monitoring Wearable

Product Overview

Our health-monitoring smart ring, built on a flexible Mylar substrate, offers cutting-edge features. Learn more about the design evolution of our smart ring.

  • Heart rate and SPO2 monitoring with a red LED sensor.
  • 6-axis motion tracking (3 accelerometers, 3 gyroscopes) at 16-bit resolution, 100 Hz sample rate.
  • 48x64 monochrome display for user interaction.
  • Wireless induction charging with a 60 mAh battery.
  • 3D-printed titanium shell (24 mm diameter) with a soft inner sleeve.
